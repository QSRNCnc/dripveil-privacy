Dripveil Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026

Summary. Dripveil is designed to work entirely on your device. Tasks, skins, tones, log rows, and preferences do not require a network connection or an account.

Data stored on your device

  • Active task, custom tasks, drop skin, and finish tone selection
  • Log entries (planned/actual duration, notes, timestamps)
  • Haptics, ringtone, and keep-awake preferences
  • Guide completion flag and starred technique notes

Data not collected

  • No account identifiers
  • No analytics or advertising SDKs
  • No location, camera, microphone, contacts, or HealthKit access
  • No sale of personal information

Permissions

Dripveil does not require camera, microphone, contacts, location, or Photo Library access for core features. Audio is used only for the optional local finish ringtone played on device.

Network

Core features work offline after install. This privacy policy is a bundled local document. The in-app reader does not load remote pages.

Children

Dripveil is a general productivity utility and is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

Clear the log, restore house tasks, change preferences, or delete the app to remove local data.

Contact

Use the App Store listing support contact for Dripveil.
